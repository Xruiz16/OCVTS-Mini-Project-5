El Yunque

A lush mountain rainforest of misty ridgelines, dense tropical greenery, and flowing water. Come for a refreshing change of pace and explore the forest at a pace that suits you.

Into the forest

Rainforest trails

Choose a walk through the forest to experience the towering trees, tropical plants, and sounds of the mountains.

Waterfalls & streams

Look for cascades and clear mountain streams along the forest's scenic routes and trails.

Take it slow

Bring water and rain protection, wear sturdy shoes, and check current forest conditions before setting out.
