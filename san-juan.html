North coast · Puerto Rico

San Juan

A vibrant capital where centuries of history meet everyday island life. Wander pastel streets, take in ocean views from historic forts, and make time for the city's lively food and music scenes.

Back to the destination card ↗

A few ways to explore

Old San Juan

Follow the blue cobblestone streets past colorful colonial buildings, plazas, cafés, and small local shops.

Historic forts

Visit the dramatic coastal fortifications of Castillo San Felipe del Morro and Castillo San Cristóbal.

City rhythms

Pair a day of history with Puerto Rican food, live music, and a stroll along the waterfront.
