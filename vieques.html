Eastern isles · Puerto Rico

Vieques

An easygoing island escape known for quiet beaches, open landscapes, and wild horses. After sunset, its famous bioluminescent bay offers a memorable way to experience the island after dark.

Quiet beaches

Spend a slow day by the water and explore the island's varied stretches of coastline.

Bioluminescent bay

See the glowing water of Mosquito Bay on a guided night visit; conditions vary with weather and moonlight.

Wild island life

Look out for Vieques' free-roaming horses and enjoy the relaxed pace of its small island communities.
